Where do the price examples come from?

Sindh: published Karachi–Hyderabad benchmark of PKR 8,000–10,000 for a pickup class up to one tonne. Punjab: a 2025 rate list shows PKR 15,000 for a one-tonne Suzuki from Lahore to Okara or Sahiwal. Neither is a confirmed 500 kg farm quote. Shared rates are fictional; replace the dedicated estimate with your own quote.
