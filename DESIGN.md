# Fasal Ki Raah interface

The supplied produce-market reference sets the direction for this farm transport planner. Use forest green for the page, lime for the primary action, and warm paper for route cards and forms.

## Typography and layout

Use the local sans-serif stack in `fasal/static/fasal/planner.css`. Set the main headline in two short lines beside the produce crate. Keep forms and prices left-aligned. On phones, stack the hero, show route photographs as thumbnails and place comparison results below the form.

## Components

Keep the wheat mark in the shared navigation. Link example route cards directly to their planner presets. Reserve lime for actions, the headline accent and the capacity graphic. Use dark text on paper for long reading, forms and booking details. Give keyboard focus a visible outline.

## Content and imagery

Use Pakistani locations, kg and PKR. Label fictional shipments at the point of use. Keep photography credits accessible beside the route cards. Use real booking statuses and distinguish historical crop prices from freight quotes.
