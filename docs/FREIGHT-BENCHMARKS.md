## What the planner calculates

A 150 kg load uses 30% of a 500 kg pickup, leaving 350 kg unused if the farmer hires it alone.

- **Punjab demo:** PKR 5,000 is an editable, fictional dedicated quote. At the fictional PKR 10/kg shared rate, 150 kg costs PKR 1,500 in freight: PKR 3,500 less (70%). The same rate would give two 75 kg batches a total of PKR 1,500 only if both trips accept the rate and no separate minimum, pickup, or handling fee applies.
- **Sindh demo:** PKR 9,000 is the midpoint of the published Karachi-to-Hyderabad benchmark used as an editable scenario input, not an exact quote for the hypothetical 500 kg vehicle. The fictional shared rate of PKR 20/kg gives PKR 3,000 in freight, PKR 6,000 below that baseline.

These are freight comparisons only. Fees and unsold produce affect what the farmer takes home. Warehouse delivery assumes a later sale and may add storage fees. The shared rate is not a guaranteed discount, and the demo never implies a dedicated 500 kg pickup can carry a heavier load in one trip.

## Contact and confirmation

A compatible trip lets the farmer draft a shipment inquiry. They can copy it or enter a known carrier's Pakistan mobile number and review the prefilled message in WhatsApp. The fictional listings contain no driver numbers. The app does not contact anyone automatically or reserve capacity. Confirm the date, load, quote, and pickup point with a real carrier before dispatch.
